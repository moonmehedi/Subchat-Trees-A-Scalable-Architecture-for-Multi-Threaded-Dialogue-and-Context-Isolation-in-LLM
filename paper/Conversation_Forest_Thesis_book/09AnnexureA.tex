\subsection{Relevant Specifications and Engineering Conventions}
The prototype uses UTF-8, structured JSON, version control, FastAPI, Next.js, Server-Sent Events and recorded clean-state experiments. It is not certified for safety-critical deployment.

\section{Design Constraints}

\subsection{Ethical Considerations}
The evaluation uses anonymized public data. Deployments must disclose inherited context, support deletion and avoid replacing professional judgment.

\subsection{Environmental Considerations}
Inference, embeddings and retrieval consume energy. Bounded buffers, reusable embeddings and conditional retrieval reduce avoidable computation.

\subsection{Social Constraints}
Explicit branching can improve navigation but adds cognitive effort. The design therefore preserves familiar chat interaction and treats branching as optional.

\subsection{Sustainability in Environmental and Societal Context}
The project supports SDG~4 through structured learning, SDG~9 through conversational-system innovation and SDG~12 through responsible computational-resource use.
